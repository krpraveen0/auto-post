## Required Questions

1. What would mislead a beginner?
2. What would a senior practitioner dispute or qualify?
3. Which claim lacks a primary source or reproducible observation?
4. Which abstraction is introduced before the reader has earned it?
5. Which example works only on the happy path?
6. What appears original but is already explained better elsewhere?
7. Can every empirical result be regenerated from committed artifacts?
8. Does each visual reduce a real reasoning burden?

## Score Discipline

Scores of 8/10 or higher require exact artifact evidence. A score of 10/10 must
state why no material improvement is currently known. A missing required
artifact forces a failing score for its category; averages cannot hide critical
failures.
