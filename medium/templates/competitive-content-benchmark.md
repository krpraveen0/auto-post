## Original Contribution

The new article contributes at least one of:

- a reproducible implementation or experiment;
- a failure analysis based on observed behavior;
- a decision framework with explicit trade-offs;
- a visual model that makes a difficult relationship inspectable;
- a better-scaffolded exercise with feedback and transfer;
- a source-verified synthesis that resolves conflicting explanations.

## Decision

- Proceed: the article has defensible original value.
- Reframe: the topic is useful, but the present angle adds little.
- Stop: a stronger existing resource already fulfills the same promise.

Reason:

This benchmark supports editorial judgment. It cannot prove that an article is
universally superior, and it must never be used to imitate another author's work.
